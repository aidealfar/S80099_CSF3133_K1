History and Background

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It officially began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science, located at Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu. It was established to provide facilities for both students and lecturers from the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes to conduct their practical sessions and research.

The Faculty of Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in Serdang was eventually transferred to Kuala Terengganu, and the branch campus was renamed Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT) in May 1996. On 5 May 1999, the Cabinet of Malaysia approved the establishment of Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) as an associate campus of UPM.

KUT was given autonomy on 1 May 2001 and renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) on 20 June 2001. Finally, on 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was elevated to the status of a full-fledged university and renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT).

Key Facts & Faculties

UMT specializes in marine and aquatic studies, supported by various dedicated faculties:

Faculty Core Specializations Established
Faculty of Science and Marine Environment Marine Biology, Chemical Sciences, Biodiversity 2001
Faculty of Fisheries and Food Science Aquaculture, Food Tech, Agrotechnology 2001
Faculty of Maritime Studies Maritime Management, Nautical Science, Logistics 2007
Faculty of Ocean Engineering Technology and Informatics Maritime Engineering, Computer Science, Data Analytics 2019
